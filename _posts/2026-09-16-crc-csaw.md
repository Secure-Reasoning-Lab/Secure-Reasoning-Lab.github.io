We are hosting the inaugural **Cyber Reasoning Challenge at CSAW (CRC@CSAW 2026)**, a student competition on autonomous vulnerability discovery and repair. Teams build cyber reasoning systems that find vulnerabilities in real software, demonstrate them with a proof of vulnerability, and generate patches that fix the bugs while preserving program functionality. See the [official CSAW event page](https://csaw.io/competition/cyber-reasoning-challenge) for registration and competition details, and the [CRC@CSAW GitHub repository](https://github.com/Secure-Reasoning-Lab/CRC-CSAW) for technical resources and updates.
