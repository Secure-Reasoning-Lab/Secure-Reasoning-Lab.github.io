The Secure Reasoning Lab is recruiting **self-motivated Ph.D. students and postdocs** to work on large language models and cybersecurity.

## Who we look for

You do not need to have done all of the following, but a strong fit usually includes some of:

- Interest in LLMs, agents, or reinforcement learning, especially for security or software engineering
- Experience with systems security, program analysis, fuzzing, or program repair
- Solid programming and experimental skills
- Curiosity about both attacking and defending AI systems

## How to apply

Email Prof. Jiahao Yu at [jy5951@nyu.edu](mailto:jy5951@nyu.edu) with:

1. A short note on why you want to join SRL and what you want to work on
2. CV
3. Transcript (students)
4. Representative papers or project links, if you have them

Please put `[SRL Application]` in the subject line.

Ph.D. applicants should also follow the [NYUAD graduate admissions](https://nyuad.nyu.edu/en/academics/graduate.html) process for Computer Engineering / relevant programs.
