The **Secure Reasoning Lab (SRL)** is a research group in the Department of Computer Engineering at [New York University Abu Dhabi](https://nyuad.nyu.edu/), led by [Prof. Jiahao Yu](https://hubertyoo.github.io/).

We work at the intersection of large language models, reinforcement learning, and cybersecurity. The lab builds AI systems that can *reason about software security* — finding vulnerabilities, generating patches, and remaining robust when those same models are attacked or misused.

## Research Focus

- **Cyber reasoning systems.** Automated vulnerability discovery and program repair, including LLM agents for patch generation and ML-guided fuzzing.
- **Secure and robust LLMs.** Jailbreak evaluation, prompt-injection testing, and alignment methods that hold up under adversarial use.
- **Reasoning and explainability.** Improving, and explaining, the critical steps that LLM and RL systems take on security tasks.
- **Blockchain security.** Detecting anomalous transactions and securing decentralized systems with learning-based methods.
